How I manage social

From the content plan to the monthly review.

Each stage has a purpose, an owner and a clear output, so content does not stop at posting.

Audit & direction→Content pillars→Calendar & briefs→Creative production→Scheduling→Community & reporting

Plan

Review the brand, audience, competitors and goals. Build content pillars, monthly themes and a practical publishing calendar.

Create & publish

Prepare captions, graphics, short-form video briefs and platform-ready assets. Schedule content and check links, tags and formatting.

Monitor & improve

Review reach, views, engagement, profile activity and content patterns. Turn observations into the next round of content decisions.
